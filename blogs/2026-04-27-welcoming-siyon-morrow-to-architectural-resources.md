Growth is especially meaningful when it creates new opportunities to better serve the people and projects we care about most. At Architectural Resources, we take pride in building a team that reflects our values and strengthens the experience we provide to clients, vendors, and industry partners. We are excited to welcome Siyon Morrow as our new Business Development Manager. In this role, she will focus on expanding client relationships and exploring new opportunities within the architectural and design community.     Siyon brings a solid foundation in project coordination, communication, operations, and process improvement. She earned her MBA from California State University, Long Beach , and has contributed to fast-moving civil engineering projects through documentation, workflow development, presentations, stakeholder communication, event coordination, and team collaboration. Her background reflects the organization, adaptability, and professionalism needed to keep projects aligned and relationships strong.     At Architectural Resources, we believe project guidance goes beyond providing products. It is built through collaboration, responsiveness, technical expertise, and a genuine commitment to service. As a San Diego-based representative agency, we are proud to support the architectural and design community throughout Southern California, serving architects, designers, contractors, manufacturers, and project teams with thoughtful guidance and trusted product solutions. Bringing the right people onto our team is an important part of that commitment.     What makes Siyon such a strong fit for Architectural Resources is her ability to bring both structure and clarity to complex, fast-moving environments. She has experience improving workflows, coordinating communication, developing presentations, and maintaining organized systems that contribute to better outcomes. Her people-centered approach to relationship-building aligns naturally with our dedication to partnership, service, and thoughtful project guidance.     We are pleased to welcome Siyon to Architectural Resources and look forward to the value she will bring to our clients, vendors, and partners. As we continue to grow, we remain committed to delivering exceptional customer service, collaborative guidance, and architectural product solutions that help move projects forward with clarity and confidence.
